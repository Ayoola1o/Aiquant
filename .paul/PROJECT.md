# Aiquant

## What This Is

Aiquant is an AI-powered Quantitative Trading Platform and Backtesting Engine. It features a Python backend (`adk_agent`, backtest engine, feature engineering lab, strategy execution) and a React/TypeScript frontend (AI Strategy Lab, Live Session, Data Management, AI Command Center).

## Core Value

Empowers quantitative traders and AI agents to design, backtest, analyze, and execute high-frequency and algorithmic trading strategies across crypto (Hyperliquid) and equities (Alpaca/Yahoo).

## Requirements

### Core Features

- **Backtest Engine**: Fast backtesting of quantitative trading strategies with customizable technical indicators and historical market data.
- **AI Strategy Lab**: Interactive UI for designing, testing, and managing algorithmic trading strategies.
- **Hyperliquid & Alpaca Integrations**: Real-time market data ingestion, order placement, and risk supervision.
- **Feature Engineering**: Volatility, momentum, and custom signal calculation modules.
