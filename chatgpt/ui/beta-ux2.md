# Institutional Workspace Features

The entire platform should support capabilities usually found only in high-end trading terminals:

* **Dockable panels** – every widget can be moved, resized, floated, or saved into custom layouts.
* **Multi-monitor support** – open separate windows for charts, AI analysis, order management, or research.
* **Workspace presets** – "Research", "Execution", "Portfolio", "AI Lab", "Scalping", "Swing", and user-defined layouts.
* **Command Palette** (Ctrl/Cmd + K) – search symbols, run AI commands, navigate instantly, or launch actions.
* **Global search** – search strategies, trades, research notes, AI conversations, indicators, datasets, and symbols from one place.
* **Keyboard-first workflow** – power users should be able to navigate almost the entire application without touching the mouse.
* **Persistent AI context** – the AI always knows the active workspace, selected symbol, timeframe, open positions, and current research so responses remain contextual.
* **Collaboration-ready architecture** – comments, shared research notebooks, strategy reviews, approvals, and version history for future team use.
* **Saved workspace snapshots** – capture the exact state of charts, panels, AI discussions, and filters to return to later or share with collaborators.

## The End Goal

The finished product should not feel like "an AI trading website." It should feel like **Bloomberg Terminal meets TradingView, enhanced with an AI research scientist, an institutional execution terminal, and a quantitative development environment**—all inside a single cohesive operating system.

If we build to that standard, the platform will stand apart from almost every retail AI trading application currently available.
